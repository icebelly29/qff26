EV charging scheduler

3 EVs, 2 stations (1 bay each), 5 hourly slots. Each toggle is one qubit: “this EV starts charging at this station in this slot.” A 14-bit string is a complete schedule.

QAOA circuit (14 qubits)

QAOA results & depth progression

All 18 feasible schedules, ranked (of 16,384 bitstrings)

Cost units are synthetic. J is the true objective (energy, travel, waiting, plus a peak-load term). H adds the penalties for breaking a rule, so the lowest H is the best feasible schedule. Both classical heuristics are feasible but achieve worse J than the QAOA optimum.
